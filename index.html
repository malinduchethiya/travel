Top Destinations

From ancient ruins to golden beaches and misty highlands.

Sigiriya

The iconic Lion Rock fortress. Climb at sunrise for unforgettable views.

Ella & Tea Country

Train rides through misty hills, Nine Arches Bridge, and lush tea plantations.

Galle Fort

Colonial charm, cobblestone streets, boutique cafes and ocean views.

Mirissa & South Coast

Whale watching, surf breaks and palm-fringed beaches.

Yala National Park

Leopard safaris and diverse wildlife in Sri Lanka's premier reserve.

Kandy

Temple of the Tooth, cultural dance shows and the scenic lake.

Visa & Entry

Requirements for travellers from the UK and India.

UK United Kingdom

ETA required before travel. Apply online via ETA portal. Valid 30 days, extendable. Passport valid 6 months. Return ticket and funds proof may be requested.

India India

ETA required. Indian nationals can apply online. 30-day tourist visa, extendable. Passport valid 6 months. Frequent flights from Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru.

General Tips

Carry printed ETA approval. Travel insurance recommended. Yellow fever certificate if arriving from endemic country. Customs: 2L alcohol, 200 cigarettes duty-free.

Flights

Direct and connecting routes.

UK From UK

Direct flights from London Heathrow (SriLankan Airlines, ~11h). Also via Dubai, Doha, Abu Dhabi with Emirates, Qatar, Etihad.

India From India

Direct flights from Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi (2.5–4h). Airlines: SriLankan, IndiGo, Air India.

Airports

Bandaranaike International (CMB) near Colombo is the main gateway. Mattala (HRI) serves south coast.
