Visa & entry

Everything UK and Indian travellers need before boarding.

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

ETA required before travel. Apply online.

  • 30-day tourist visa, extendable
  • Passport valid 6 months
  • Return ticket & funds proof
  • Direct flights ~11h from LHR
🇮🇳

India

ETA required. Apply online.

  • 30-day tourist visa, extendable
  • Passport valid 6 months
  • Flights from Chennai, Mumbai, Delhi, BLR
  • Duration 2.5–4 hours
✈️

Pro tips

Smooth arrival checklist.

  • Carry printed ETA approval
  • Travel insurance recommended
  • Yellow fever cert if applicable
  • Duty-free: 2L alcohol, 200 cigs
